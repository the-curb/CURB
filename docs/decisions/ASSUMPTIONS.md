## How the register is used

- *What the site says meanwhile* is checked against the pages whenever the register changes.
- A replaced assumption records who, when and what; its decision record moves from *proposed* to *decided*.
- Work independent of open questions goes on: the path from a reviewed record to a holder's own wallet was rehearsed with no assumption promoted.
