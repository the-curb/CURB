## 0. What changes and what does not

Unchanged (TOKEN.md, decided 12 September): the price is the pool's own at a block; market cap = price × `totalSupply()`; a top-up is credited at its own block by state, else by the pool's events, else — only when the pool definitely had no price at that block — at the head when indexed, and the credit says which; the guard credits at the lower of the block's price and the lowest price in the window before it (35,000 blocks on chain 4663, `GUARD_WINDOW_BLOCKS`); no price is typed in; a bonding-curve period is a period with no conversion (rule 5).

New: a third `priceSource.kind`, `uniswap-v4-pool`. A v4 pool has no address (PRIMARY: v4-core `PoolKey.sol`/`PoolId.sol`; PoolManager stores no keys). Its identity is the PoolKey and `poolId = keccak256(abi.encode(currency0, currency1, fee, tickSpacing, hooks))`, and every event of the pool is emitted by the singleton PoolManager `0x8366a39cc670b4001a1121b8f6a443a643e40951` with `poolId` as topic1 (PRIMARY: Uniswap deployments page "Robinhood Chain: 4663" + `Uniswap/contracts` deployments/json/4663.json; live Initialize/Swap/ModifyLiquidity logs decoded on chain). State is read through StateView `0xf3334192d15450cdd385c8b70e03f9a6bd9e673b` (`StateView.poolManager()` = the PoolManager; `getSlot0` equals raw `extsload` on the PoolManager for two pools — PRIMARY). No per-pool `token0()/token1()/slot0()/liquidity()` exists; the current `sides()` and `poolPriceByState()` cannot be pointed at any v4 address (PRIMARY: reader gap 3).

## 1. Constants the reader adds

Topics (PRIMARY: computed from the verified ABIs and seen in live receipts):
- `Swap(bytes32 indexed id, address indexed sender, int128 amount0, int128 amount1, uint160 sqrtPriceX96, uint128 liquidity, int24 tick, uint24 fee)` → `0x40e9cecb9f5f1f1c5b9c97dec2917b7ee92e57ba5563708daca94dd84ad7112f`; data words [0] amount0, [1] amount1, [2] sqrtPriceX96 after, [3] liquidity after, [4] tick, [5] fee. Words [2] and [3] sit where the v3 decoder already reads them.
- `Initialize(bytes32 indexed id, address indexed currency0, address indexed currency1, uint24 fee, int24 tickSpacing, address hooks, uint160 sqrtPriceX96, int24 tick)` → `0xdd466e674ea557f56295e2d0218a125ea4b4f0f6f3307b95f85e6110838d6438`; data words [0] fee, [1] tickSpacing, [2] hooks, [3] sqrtPriceX96, [4] tick.
- `ModifyLiquidity(bytes32 indexed id, address indexed sender, int24 tickLower, int24 tickUpper, int256 liquidityDelta, bytes32 salt)` → `0xf208f4912782fd25c7f114ca3723a2d5dd6f3bcc3ac8db5af63baa85f711d5ec`; replaces v3 Mint (positive delta) and Burn (negative delta).

Selectors (PRIMARY: computed; answered live): StateView `getSlot0(bytes32)` = `0xc815641c` → (sqrtPriceX96, tick, protocolFee, lpFee); `getLiquidity(bytes32)` = `0xfa6793d5` → uint128; `poolManager()` = `0xdc4c90d3`. ## 2. `sides()` for v4 — from the key, no pool calls

- `curbIs0 = key.currency0 == config.token`, else `key.currency1 == config.token`, else the record is CONFIG_INVALID at parse time (no RPC). Rests on: the factory sorts `(pairToken < memecoin ? pairToken : memecoin)` as currency0 (PRIMARY: verified `_sortCurrencies`), so native ETH (`address(0)`) is always currency0 and an ERC-20 quote may be either side — `hook.launches(poolId).memecoinIsCurrency0` says which (PRIMARY: 13-word LaunchInfo decode).
- `quoteAddress` = the other currency. If it is `0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000` it is native ETH: `quoteDecimals = 18`, no `decimals()` call (there is no code at address 0). `decodeAddressWord` returns `null` for the zero word (PRIMARY: `lib/chain/abi.ts` line 71; reader gap 2) — the v4 decoder must decode currency words with a zero-tolerant helper and never treat address(0) as "absent".
- CURB decimals from `config.token.decimals()` at the head as today (PonsV2LauncherToken is stock OpenZeppelin ERC20 → 18; FOMOBALL `decimals()` = 18 — PRIMARY).
- Quote decimals for an ERC-20 quote from `decimals()` at the head as today (USDG 6 — PRIMARY: `pairTokenEconomics(USDG).decimals` = 6 and `decimals()`).

## 3. Price by state (the tick at the head; a top-up inside the node's state window)

`eth_call StateView.getSlot0(poolId)` and `getLiquidity(poolId)` at the block → `PoolPrice { kind: 'uniswap-v4-pool', sqrtPriceX96, liquidity }`; `quotePerCurb18` is the existing v3 arithmetic (`sqrtP²/2¹⁹²` = currency1 per currency0; inverted when CURB is currency1). Zero liquidity or zero sqrtPrice = no price, as for v3. The node serves state only ~6,000–10,000 blocks back (head−6,000 answered, head−10,000 refused "historical state … is not available" — PRIMARY, 17 September), so the tick's head read is by state and most top-ups go by events, exactly as today (`STATE_GONE` regex already matches "state … not available").

## 4. Price by events (a top-up's own block)

`lastPriceEventBefore` for v4: the last log with `address = PoolManager, topics = [Swap, poolId]` at or before the block, widening through `EVENT_WINDOWS` down to `fromBlock` (the Initialize block); price = word[2], liquidity = word[3]. If the Swap left liquidity 0, a later `ModifyLiquidity` with positive `liquidityDelta` (topics `[ModifyLiquidity, poolId]`) at or before the block means liquidity is present again (same rule as v3 Mint). Before the first Swap: the `Initialize` by poolId gives the first price (word[3]), valid only with a positive-delta `ModifyLiquidity` at or before the block; positive then negative delta with no Swap since = `liquidityAfter: 'UNCERTAIN'` (the v3 rule, signed deltas replacing Mint/Burn). The Initialize-sanity check (v3: `slot0()` at head vs the Initialize price when no Swap is known) becomes `StateView.getSlot0(poolId)` at head vs the Initialize's sqrtPriceX96. Rests on: a PONS graduation emits Initialize (logIndex 0x6) and the full-range mint ModifyLiquidity (logIndex 0xc, sender PositionManager, −887200/887200, +29277002188455996397858) in the same transaction, in that order (PRIMARY: receipt 0x34072a39…, re-read today with `[null, poolId]` over the Initialize block: exactly those two logs).

`readLogs` already takes a `topics` array; `lastInRange`, `lastEventBefore` and `foldEventsInRange` take one topic today and must accept `[topic0, poolId]`.

## 5. The guard window from Swap events by poolId

For a block B: fold `eth_getLogs {address: PoolManager, topics: [Swap, poolId], fromBlock: max(fromBlock, B − 35,000), toBlock: B}` page by page in (block, logIndex) order, taking the minimum USD price of word[2] with word[3] ≠ 0, plus the opening price (the last Swap before the window, else the Initialize when it lies inside the window and liquidity followed by B) — the existing `assemble` logic with the address/topic substitution. Rules that rest on facts:
- Count swaps from every sender, the hook included: the hook's own fee-conversion (and, if enabled, buyback) swaps are real trades that move the price and appear as ordinary Swap events with `sender == hook` (PRIMARY: 14 of 1,866 and 6 of 1,400 sample swaps; verified hook `unlockCallback`).
- Never derive a price from amount0/amount1: they are the pool's gross deltas before the hook skim (PRIMARY: HookFeeCollected feeAmount = exactly 1% of Swap.amount1 in two receipts). Use sqrtPriceX96 only.
- The Swap `fee` word is 0 on every PONS pool (static PoolKey.fee 0, hook has no beforeSwap) — recorded, not acted on (PRIMARY: 3,774 + 4,224 + 1,400 sample events, all fee 0).
- Page budget: one hot pool produced 3,588 swaps in the 11.7 minutes after graduation and 8,707 in 57 minutes (8.24 MB in one answer) — under the node's 10,000-log cap in most 35,000-block windows, above it on a hotter launch, so halving on "logs matched by query exceeds limit of 10000" (already matched by `isTooManyLogs`) and `GUARD_MAX_PAGES = 64` stay as they are (PRIMARY, 16–17 September). Today's window on the FOMOBALL pool held 4 swaps (blocks 64,817,265–64,822,042, all sender Universal Router 0x8876…0904, liquidity constant, fee 0) — PRIMARY, read at head 64,850,391.
- Liquidity liveness: the graduation position is one full-range NFT minted to the Launch Locker, which exposes no withdrawal; third parties may add liquidity (ORBIO's pool liquidity exceeds its locked position) — the reader keeps the generic zero-liquidity rule and needs nothing PONS-specific (PRIMARY: `ownerOf` = locker; locker source; ORBIO reads).

2. `… fromBlock: fromBlock, toBlock: fromBlock` must hold exactly one Initialize whose topic2/topic3/data reproduce the record's key and whose topic1 equals the locally recomputed `keccak256(abi.encode(key))` (the project's `lib/chain/keccak.ts` reproduced two live poolIds — PRIMARY).

## 7. What the hook can do to a quote, and how the reader treats it

Hook `0xE5e702641Ea86F4ae6cC3cDaeD2B886f976Be044`, permissions from its address bits 0x2044 = beforeInitialize, afterSwap, afterSwapReturnDelta only (PRIMARY: `getHookPermissions()` on chain; source). Consequences:
- It cannot move `sqrtPriceX96`, `tick` or `liquidity` inside a swap and cannot set an LP fee (no beforeSwap; fee is static 0, `CoreLpFeeMustBeZero`) — the Swap event and slot0 are the pool's true marginal state (PRIMARY: v4-core Pool.swap / LPFeeLibrary / Hooks.sol; all sample fees 0).
- After each swap it takes `hookFeeBps` (100 = 1%, snapshotted at launch) + `creatorTaxBps` (0–1,000) of the UNSPECIFIED leg via `poolManager.take` and returns it as a delta; a buyer of CURB with ETH receives 1% (+ creator tax) less CURB than the pool's own delta; a seller receives 1% (+ tax) less ETH (PRIMARY: `_afterSwap`; HookFeeCollected = 1% of amount1; sell fee taken in ETH in tx 0x2c2188e2…). The desk's quote is the crediting rate, not the cost of acquiring CURB: the services page must say that a buyer pays the hook's skim on top, and state the launch's `hookFeeBps` and `creatorTaxBps` read from `hook.launches(poolId)` (selector `0xad091230`, 13 words: registered, memecoinIsCurrency0, memecoin, quoteToken, creator, buybackCreatorRecipient, protocolFeeRecipient, creatorTaxBps, protocolFeeShareBps, buybackBurnBps, hookFeeBps, maxInternalPriceImpactBps, buybackEnabled — PRIMARY, decoded on chain; a 12-word decoder misaligns). The skim is informational: it never enters `usdPerCurb18`.
- Its own swaps (fee conversion under `maxInternalPriceImpactBps` = 300, operator-only; buybacks if the creator enabled them) move the price and are weighed by the guard like any trade (PRIMARY). With `buybackEnabled = false` (the record's no-buyback line, TOKEN.md) only fee conversions occur.
- Owner powers on an existing pool are fee-side only: `rescuePoolFees` (onlyOwner), `setFeeSweepOperator`; policy changes apply to future launches (snapshotted); no pause; not upgradeable (EIP-1967 slots zero) (PRIMARY: verified hook source; storage reads). Nothing the hook owner can do changes a price the reader reads; the reader needs no hook-owner logic.

## 8. The curve period (pre-graduation) — no conversion, the decided term

- On the curve the token trades on `PonsV2BondingCurve` (buy/sell; `getReserves()` includes the 1.68 ETH phantom reserve; `CurveBuy`/`CurveSell` events; no Sync) — the reader never prices from it (TOKEN.md rule 5). No curve adapter is built. Protection that already exists: the curve reuses the v2-pair selector `getReserves()` = `0x0902f1ac` but returns `(uint256,uint256)` and answers no `token0()/token1()`, so a `uniswap-v2-pair` record naming the curve fails `sides()` (UNREAD) and the dry run's `token0()` check (PRIMARY: verified curve source; selector computed).
- `priceSource` stays `null` until the Initialize exists: the record refuses a `fromBlock` past the head, and `fromBlock` is the Initialize block. With `priceSource: null` the tick writes "no pool is recorded for the token; nothing is quoted until one is", `/services` composes no top-up bytes (`topUpReadiness.topUp === null`, `topUpHeld` set) and the page must add, in the curve period, that the token trades on a PONS curve which the desk does not read.
- Phase is read from `factory.getLaunchedToken(token).phase` (0 NotGraduated / 1 Swept / 2 PoolCreated / 3 Rescued; struct order token, curve, deployer, creatorFeeRecipient, pairToken, graduationThreshold, poolFee, tickSpacing, creatorTaxBps, buybackEnabled, phase, sweptQuote, sweptTokens, sweptAt, exists — PRIMARY: `ILaunchpadV2.sol` in the Sourcify bundle, read today). Only phase 2 yields a pool; phase 3 (owner rescue after 7 days in Swept) means no pool ever.

## 9. A top-up made during the curve

The CreditDesk has no pause and the launch token is a plain ERC-20 (no transfer tax, no blacklist — PRIMARY: OZ v5.5.0 ERC20 + ERC20Burnable byte-identical; hook "does not tax transfers"), so a holder who bought on the curve can call `topUp(keyHash, amount)` before any pool exists and `DeltaWrong` will not fire. What the code does today, and what this spec keeps: (a) the indexer reads the TopUp and lists it "waits for a pool to be recorded; priced then", attempts 0, never credited at a curve price; (b) once the v4 source is configured with `fromBlock` = Initialize block, `assemble` answers `NO_PRICE_AT_BLOCK … it was created in block <fromBlock>` for that top-up's block, `poolHadNoPriceAt` is true, and the indexer credits it at the head at indexing (`basis: 'HEAD_AT_INDEXING'`, guard applied over the hour before that head), the credit saying so — TOKEN.md rule 2's "created later" branch, decided 12 September. Consequence to state on `/services` during the curve: a top-up sent now is credited at whatever the graduated pool shows at the first successful tick after the pool is recorded — in the worked example the pool's price fell 95% within 13 minutes of graduation — and if the launch never graduates the CURB is at the treasury and the credit never prices. Whether to keep that decided reading or to hold curve-period top-ups for an explicit product-owner decision is open question 6.

## 10. Node behaviour the reader must absorb before v4 works (measured, PRIMARY)

- `lib/chain/logs.ts` `isTooManyLogs()` must also match `log query timed out` and `context deadline exceeded`: the public node returns them as JSON-RPC `-32000` with HTTP 200 on wide PoolManager queries, nondeterministically (the same 2M-block window answered and timed out on different attempts; today the wildcard whole-chain query timed out 3/3). `rpc.ts` maps them to `SOURCE_MALFORMED` and `halveable()` says no, so `lastInRange`/`foldEventsInRange` fault instead of halving (reader gap 1, regex tested).
- HTTP/JSON 429 "Too Many Requests" follows a burst or a timed-out query (three consecutive 429s today after two timeouts; one 429 despite 2 s spacing on 17 September): treat as retryable with a pause (never halve on it — halving multiplies calls), and pace PoolManager queries ≥ 2 s apart within a tick.
- Always send an explicit `toBlock` (the head read at the start of the run), as the reader already does through `hexBlock`.
- The dRPC keyed endpoint the tick reads through caps a query at 100,000 blocks and has no result cap (measured 13 September, `logs.ts` header): the 35,000-block guard window fits in one query; the whole-chain step of `checkPoolInitialize` pages there, as `checkPoolCreation` does today.

### Field rules (`parseCreditsConfig`)
- `kind`: `'uniswap-v2-pair' | 'uniswap-v3-pool' | 'uniswap-v4-pool'`.
- `pair`: 20-byte address = the PoolManager. `stateView`: 20-byte address. Both required for the v4 kind.
- `poolId`: `0x` + 64 hex. Must equal `keccak256(abi.encode(currency0, currency1, fee, tickSpacing, hooks))` recomputed locally with `lib/chain/keccak.ts` (each field left-padded to 32 bytes; tickSpacing as two's-complement int24 in a word) — CONFIG_INVALID otherwise. Rests on PoolIdLibrary (`keccak256(poolKey, 0xa0)`) and two live reproductions (PRIMARY).
- `key.currency0 < key.currency1` numerically; exactly one of them equals `token` (lowercased) — else CONFIG_INVALID. `fee`: integer 0..1,000,000 (0x800000 dynamic sentinel refused — the reader has no dynamic-fee logic; PONS pools are fee 0). `tickSpacing`: integer 1..32767 (PONS config 0: 200). `hooks`: 20-byte address (may be the zero address for a hookless pool).

## Risks

- Terms of Use §11 (no capital raise / revenue participation / tokenized equity-or-debt / ownership, creditor or investment interest) and §3 (UK, EU and sanctioned jurisdictions excluded) govern the interface; a launch through it is an acceptance. The CURB prepaid-credit design and the published proceeds waterfall (review and legal paid from launch proceeds) need counsel's reading before a launch through the app. Direct contract launch avoids the interface Terms but pons may hide the token (§7), and 'do not imply a partnership, endorsement, or official status' applies to how THE CURB names pons (PRIMARY: ponsfamily.com/terms; docs.ponsfamily.com/#terms).
- PONS v2 is unaudited by its own statement (three reviews 'in progress', none closed; SB Security lists a v1-worded engagement with '3 Low' and no report; Pashov has no pons entry; Dingbats has no locatable publication — PRIMARY, 17 September). The published GitHub tree is not the deployed code (its curve lacks the snipe tax; deployer uses `new` instead of CREATE2; escrow and launch-and-buy sources absent) — only the Sourcify bundles describe what is deployed; the Graduation Guard and every per-launch token/curve are unverified on Sourcify; the official explorer was unreachable to automation on 16–17 September (PRIMARY).
- Graduation is not assured: 4.2 ETH of net buys (≈ US$10,086 at the ETH / USD answer read at block 64,850,391) must arrive; ≈1.4% of launches graduated in the measured window. Without graduation there is never a pool, the desk never quotes, and a top-up sent during the curve is never priced. There is no rescue for a NotGraduated launch and no way to withdraw the curve's reserves except the owner's 7-day rescue of a stuck Swept launch (PRIMARY).
- Every launch term is owner-editable between the reviewed record and the transaction (config, launch fee, gate, pair-token approval, fee policy, snipe window) and the docs disagree with the chain today (launching 'closed' vs `launchEnabled` true since at least block 56,000,000; snipe 5 s vs 3 s); the app announces a rollout. Only a same-block re-read with `expectedEconomics` pinned protects the launch; a zero digest waives it (PRIMARY: verified line 792).
- Post-graduation liquidity is thin and the price is volatile: the seed is 4.2 ETH + 204.08M tokens full-range (≈ US$20,170), the worked example fell 95% in 13 minutes and a second sample 21× in a few hours. The guard limits a short pump but not a dump (a dump before a top-up costs the payer, as TOKEN.md says) nor sustained manipulation; a pumped price held for more than an hour costs the desk. The credit desk cannot pause and offers no refund (PRIMARY: 1,400 swaps decoded; StateView reads; CreditDesk.sol).
- A curve-period top-up is credited at the graduated pool's price at the first successful tick after the pool is recorded (`HEAD_AT_INDEXING`, the decided 'created later' branch), which can be far from the curve price the payer paid; if the launch never graduates the CURB sits at the treasury unpriced. The page must say this in the curve period, or the product owner must decide otherwise (open question 6).
- The hook's skim (1% + creator tax of the output leg) makes every purchase of CURB cost more than the desk's quote implies, is invisible in the PoolManager's Swap event and can be read only from HookFeeCollected; a page that shows only the pool-marginal price misstates the payer's cost (PRIMARY: `_afterSwap`; receipts).
- Node behaviour: the public node answers wide PoolManager queries nondeterministically (`log query timed out` / `context deadline exceeded`, HTTP 200) and rate-limits with 429 after a burst; today the wildcard-topic whole-chain query failed 3/3. The current `logs.ts`/`rpc.ts` treat those as non-halveable faults (gap 1), `decodeAddressWord` drops native ETH (gap 2), and `config.ts`/`rate.ts` have no v4 kind (gap 3). None of this is fixable by configuration; the reader must change before any v4 pool prices (PRIMARY).
- The operator's machine cannot resolve `rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com` (ISP DNS hijack to 114.7.173.245/246 with a wrong certificate); every local tool run needs a network that resolves it or a pinned origin (104.20.46.209 / 172.66.147.70 per DoH). Railway is not on that path (PRIMARY, reproduced today).
- Native-ETH quote adds a Chainlink dependency (ETH / USD, heartbeat 24 h, 0.5% deviation): a stale or negative answer is 'no rate' for the block and the desk stops quoting; a USDG quote avoids the feed but USDG launches need 8,090 USDG to graduate and drew 35 of 633 launches in an hour (PRIMARY).

None affects a price the reader reads, but the treasury's fee income can be redirected — the proceeds accounting must watch `CreatorFeeRecipientChangeProposed` (PRIMARY).
