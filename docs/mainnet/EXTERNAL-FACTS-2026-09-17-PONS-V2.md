# PONS v2 on Robinhood Chain — what was read, 16–17 September 2026

**Status:** research record, read from primary sources (the operator's Terms of Use and docs, the published repository, the Sourcify-verified deployed contracts, and the chain itself through public unkeyed nodes) after the product owner decided on 17 September 2026 that the CURB token launches on PONS v2. Nothing here is a term of THE CURB's: the decided record is [the token record](../decisions/TOKEN.md); this is what the venue was found to be on those two days, so the record could be written from it and not assumed (the register's A12). Every launch term below is editable by the venue's owner between a read and a transaction — the launch procedure re-reads them in the same block it sends.

Each reader's findings were re-verified by a second, sceptical reader; their verdicts and corrections are at the end. Confidence marks: PRIMARY = the operator's own document, verified source, or a chain read; SECONDARY = press or an aggregator; UNVERIFIED = stated but not confirmed.


## PONS V2 official terms and flow (docs.ponsfamily.com/v2, app, Terms of Use), cross-checked against live chain reads on Robinhood Chain (4663) and the published source, read 16/17 September 2026

PONS v2 terms are governed by Pons Labs, LLC's Terms of Use (https://www.ponsfamily.com/terms, effective 16 July 2026, Delaware law, AAA arbitration) which apply to the interface, not the contracts: no KYC, but UK, EU and sanctioned jurisdictions are excluded and section 11 forbids creating a token 'in connection with any capital raise ... revenue participation right, tokenized equity or debt representation, or any other arrangement intended to represent an ownership, creditor, or investment interest in an ongoing business or enterprise' — this needs a product-owner/legal call before any CURB launch through the app. The protocol flow is fully documented at https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2 and every number the docs leave to code was read from the chain: launch fee 0.0005 ETH (to protocol recipient 0x263e…19Dd, who is also factory/hook/locker owner); base trade fee 1% on the curve and 1% via the hook in the pool (pool LP fee 0), split 30% protocol / 70% creator, or 30/35/35 with buybacks; optional creator tax up to 10% (all to creator, immutable); no creator allocation at all (full 1e9 supply minted to the curve; the creator's only tokens are bought, and only buyback tokens vest, over 5 years); single launch config: 1.68 ETH phantom reserve, 4.2 ETH graduation threshold, tickSpacing 200, so 28.57% of supply is reserved, the price rises 12.25x from a 1.68 ETH FDV to a 20.58 ETH FDV, and at graduation 4.2 ETH + 204.08M tokens (20.4%) seed a full-range Uniswap v4 position minted to the locker (verified: ownerOf = locker) while 81.63M tokens (8.16%) are locked forever. The pool key is {sorted currencies, fee 0, tickSpacing 200, hook 0xE5e7…e044} on Uniswap's official Robinhood Chain PoolManager 0x8366…0951 (StateView 0xf333…673b available for a reader). Quote asset is native ETH (address 0) or an approved ERC-20: USDG (3,236 / 8,090 USDG), cbBTC and about 22 Robinhood stock tokens incl. AAPL are approved; WETH is NOT approved. The launched token has no mint/pause/blacklist/tax/upgrade (ERC20 + Burnable only), the curve has no pause, and the creator retains only fee-recipient and buyback toggles; the owner can change terms for future launches, propose a fee-recipient override with a 3-day timelock, and rescue a launch stuck in Swept after 7 days. Two live discrepancies against the docs: the chain says public launching is OPEN (launchEnabled = true; canLaunch(Curb Safe) = true without a whitelist entry; 633 launches by 578 addresses in ~67 minutes) while the docs say it is closed, and the snipe-tax window is 3 s on chain versus 5 s in the docs. Audits: none published — Pashov's repo has no pons entry as of 15 Sept, SB Security's portfolio lists a Pons engagement ('3 Low', v1 wording, no report), Dingbats has no locatable publication; the docs say 'Treat v2 as unaudited'.

## v4-on-robinhood: Uniswap v4 addresses on chain 4663, poolId derivation, price read, Swap-event history, PONS hook effects, and what a v3-style lowest-price guard needs from v4 events

StateView, V4Quoter, PositionManager and the PONS hook all answer poolManager() = 0x8366…0951; the hook's factory() = the recorded PONS V2 factory 0x7eD5…EC7e.

POOL IDENTITY. A v4 pool has no address. Its key is PoolKey{currency0, currency1 (sorted numerically; native ETH is address(0) and therefore currency0), uint24 fee, int24 tickSpacing, address hooks}; poolId = keccak256 of the five fields each left-padded to 32 bytes (PoolIdLibrary: keccak256(poolKey, 0xa0)). Verified with the project's own keccak against a live PONS pool: key (0x0, 0x9f6b…d9c1, 0, 200, 0xe5e7…e044) → 0xa781cab7…e665 = the Initialize event's topic1. The PoolManager stores no keys, so the config must carry the five fields (or the reader recovers them from the Initialize log, whose block is the pool's fromBlock).

CURRENT PRICE. eth_call StateView.getSlot0(poolId) (selector 0xc815641c ‖ poolId) → (sqrtPriceX96, tick, protocolFee, lpFee); StateView.getLiquidity(poolId) (0xfa6793d5) → uint128. Equivalent without StateView: PoolManager.extsload(keccak256(abi.encodePacked(poolId, uint256(6)))) packs [lpFee|protocolFee|tick|sqrtPriceX96(160 bits)]; liquidity at that slot + 3. Price = sqrtPriceX96²/2^192 (currency1 per currency0), the same arithmetic as the reader's v3 path; 'curbIs0' comes from the key, not token0(). State is only served ~6,000 blocks back (head−3,000 answered, head−30,000 'historical state … is not available').

HISTORY. All pool events are emitted by the PoolManager with poolId as topic1, so filters are {address: PoolManager, topics: [topic0, poolId]}. Swap(PoolId indexed id, address indexed sender, int128 amount0, int128 amount1, uint160 sqrtPriceX96, uint128 liquidity, int24 tick, uint24 fee) topic0 0x40e9cecb9f5f1f1c5b9c97dec2917b7ee92e57ba5563708daca94dd84ad7112f; data words [2]=sqrtPriceX96-after, [3]=liquidity-after — the same positions the v3 decoder uses. Initialize(id, currency0, currency1 indexed; fee, tickSpacing, hooks, sqrtPriceX96, tick) topic0 0xdd466e67…6438. ModifyLiquidity(id, sender indexed; tickLower, tickUpper, int256 liquidityDelta, salt) topic0 0xf208f491…d5ec replaces Mint/Burn (positive/negative delta). The Swap `fee` is the pool's LP+protocol fee in pips actually applied; a hook can only change it via a beforeSwap override on a dynamic-fee (0x800000) pool or updateDynamicLPFee, and cannot alter sqrtPriceX96.

PONS HOOK 0xE5e7…e044. Address bits 0x2044 → beforeInitialize, afterSwap, afterSwapReturnDelta only (confirmed by getHookPermissions() on chain and by source). PONS pools are initialized with fee = 0 (CoreLpFeeMustBeZero), a full-range position minted through PositionManager to a locker; the hook restricts initialize to the factory and, after each swap, takes hookFeeBps + creatorTaxBps of the unspecified (output) leg via poolManager.take and returns it as a delta (caps 10% hook, 20% total). Effect for a reader: the Swap event's sqrtPriceX96, tick and liquidity are the pool's true post-swap state; the skim does not touch price or the event's fee field (all 3,774 sample swaps show fee 0); a taker's realised price is worse by the skim (sample pool: hookFeeBps 100 = 1%, creatorTaxBps 0; HookFeeCollected feeAmount equals exactly 1% of the Swap's amount1). The hook's own fee-conversion swaps (14 of 1,846 in the sample, sender = hook) are real trades that move the price and appear as normal Swap events. Amounts in the event are pre-skim pool deltas, so do not derive a price from amount0/amount1; use sqrtPriceX96.

LOWEST-PRICE GUARD IN V4. It needs, per pool: (1) address = PoolManager, topic0 = Swap, topic1 = poolId over [block − 35,000, block] (about an hour on 4663); (2) fold the minimum of sqrtPriceX96 (word 2) per event, in block/logIndex order, with liquidity (word 3) as the liveness check exactly as for v3; (3) Initialize as the pre-first-swap price, and ModifyLiquidity (signed) in place of Mint/Burn for the 'liquidity present/uncertain' logic; (4) include swaps from every sender, hook included; (5) a page budget sized for launchpad volume — one fresh PONS pool produced ~3,800 swaps (~3.5 MB of JSON) in 12 minutes, so the 10,000-log cap and GUARD_MAX_PAGES = 64 hold but answers are large; (6) tolerance for the node's nondeterministic -32000 'context deadline exceeded'/'log query timed out' on wide spans of the PoolManager address (all spans up to the whole chain answered in 5–7 s with an explicit toBlock on some attempts and timed out on others; use an explicit head block, not 'latest', and pace calls ≥2 s apart to avoid 429). Three concrete reader changes follow: (a) logs.ts halveable()/isTooManyLogs() must recognise 'context deadline exceeded' and 'log query timed out' (today they become SOURCE_MALFORMED and abort instead of halving); (b) abi.ts decodeAddressWord returns null for address(0), which in v4 is native ETH — the currency0 of nearly every PONS pool (18 decimals, no decimals() call); (c) config.ts/rate.ts need a new kind (e.g. 'uniswap-v4-pool') carrying poolManager, stateView, the five PoolKey fields and the Initialize block, with sides() taken from the key and state reads routed to StateView, since token0()/slot0()/liquidity() do not exist on any v4 address. No CURB pool exists; nothing here is a launch or configuration decision.

**Blockers noted by this reader:**
- This machine's ISP resolver hijacks rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com to 114.7.173.245/246 (nginx 405 page, wrong-principal certificate); every local chain read needs the real origin pinned (curl --resolve …:443:172.66.147.70 from DoH); a local rehearsal of the reader against the public node will fail until the runtime resolves the name correctly.
